We are the developers of **PathBinder**, a trading-card-game collection tracker, peer-to-peer marketplace, and point-of-sale tool for card shops. We are preparing our first US App Store release and are writing to request the **External Purchase Link Entitlement** so that we can manage our subscription plans through external web checkout, consistent with Apple's current US App Store guidelines following the *Epic Games v. Apple* ruling — the same capability Apple has approved for apps such as Spotify.

We want to be precise about scope, because PathBinder has two distinct and separable revenue types:

1. **Marketplace sales of physical trading cards.** Buyers purchase real, physical goods from other users and shops, fulfilled by shipping. These are processed through Stripe today and, as physical goods and services, fall outside Apple's In-App Purchase requirement. This is **not** part of this request.

2. **Software subscription tiers** (Enthusiast, Vendor, and Shop), which unlock digital features such as listing capacity, inventory/POS tools, and analytics. This is the **only** portion of our business relevant to this request.

For these subscription tiers, we would like to:

- Display our plans and pricing inside the app, and
- Offer a link to our own external web checkout so customers can subscribe there,

with the appropriate region check so this behavior is limited to the **US storefront**, as Apple's guidelines require.

We intend to fully comply with Apple's External Purchase Link Entitlement terms, including the required disclosure sheet, the approved link format and entitlement API, applicable reporting, and any commission Apple is entitled to collect on qualifying external-link transactions under the current guidelines. We are not requesting any exception to those terms — only access to the entitlement itself.

Could you please confirm the correct process to have this entitlement granted for `gg.pathbinder.app`, and let us know if any additional business verification or documentation is required from us?
