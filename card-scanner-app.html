The scanners, briefly and honestly

Scan + track + sell

PathBinder

Scans Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and more into one collection, identifies the card, attaches a live price, and asks about finish (e.g. Normal vs Reverse Holo) so your counts stay variant-accurate. It also scans sealed product, and vendors get a point-of-sale scan mode. The payoff of scanning into PathBinder specifically is that you can then sell those cards in-app at a 6–8% fee. Free tier includes 100 scans/month with unlimited tracking. For pure bulk-scan speed, the single-game apps below are faster — the honest trade-off.

Fastest, Magic

ManaBox & Dragon Shield

If raw scanning speed is the whole job, these win. ManaBox is the fast, free Magic scanner whose results feed deck stats. Dragon Shield ships a dedicated scanner-and-collection app per game (MTG, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Pokémon, Flesh and Blood) built for ripping through bulk. Neither is a marketplace — you sell elsewhere.

Value charts

Collectr

Scan, then watch portfolio-style value charts across 25+ games. Strong if your goal is tracking what your collection is worth over time. Its shop is buy-side only — no listing your own cards to other users.

Scan then sell

TCGplayer & CollX

Both scan and let you sell. TCGplayer routes to the largest US marketplace (fees ~13%). CollX covers sports and TCG on one big database with an in-app marketplace — a good low-friction option across categories, free up to 500 items.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to scan trading cards and get their value?

For raw scanning speed on bulk lots, Dragon Shield and ManaBox. For scanning across many games into one collection you can also sell from, PathBinder. For value-tracking after scanning, Collectr. CollX and TCGplayer also scan. Most have free tiers.

Can an app identify a card from a photo?

Yes. PathBinder, ManaBox, Dragon Shield, Collectr, CollX and TCGplayer recognize a card by its art and set symbol, then add it with a current price. Accuracy is best in good light with the full card in frame.

Is there a free card scanner app?

Yes — ManaBox, Dragon Shield, Collectr, CollX and PathBinder all scan on a free tier. PathBinder's free tier includes 100 scans/month with unlimited tracking.

Which scanner works across Pokémon, Magic and One Piece?

PathBinder, Collectr, TCGplayer and CollX scan across multiple games into one collection. ManaBox is Magic-focused and Dragon Shield ships a scanner app per game.
