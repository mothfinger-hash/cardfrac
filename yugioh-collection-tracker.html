The apps, briefly and honestly

Tracking + selling

PathBinder

Tracks Yu-Gi-Oh! alongside Magic, Pokémon and One Piece in one binder, with a scanner, live prices, and a built-in marketplace where you sell your own cards at a 6–8% fee (about half of eBay/TCGplayer). Free tier is unlimited. The honest limits: PathBinder is not an official Konami app and not a deckbuilder — for deck registration, the latest banlist and rulings, use Neuron or YGOPRODeck. PathBinder is for tracking what you own and turning cards into cash, across every game you play.

Official app

Konami Neuron

Konami's free official Yu-Gi-Oh! app. It bundles the card database, deck building and registration, duel/life-point tools, and camera card recognition. It's the authoritative source for what a card does and whether it's tournament-legal — but it's a play-and-deck tool, not a collection-value portfolio, and there's no way to sell your cards through it.

Database + deckbuilding

YGOPRODeck

The big community database and deckbuilder, with card text, banlist, archetype browsing and price references. Excellent for researching cards and building lists on the web. It isn't a camera-scanner app for cataloguing your physical binder, and it points out to other sites to actually buy or sell.

Marketplace-first

TCGplayer

Primarily the largest US card marketplace, with a free collection tracker and scanner wired into it. Best if your priority is selling Yu-Gi-Oh! to the biggest buyer pool — the trade-off is seller fees around 13% and a tracker that's a feature of a store rather than the main event.

Old-school trading

Deckbox

A long-running collection, wishlist and user-to-user trade-matching site covering Yu-Gi-Oh! and other games. If you prefer classic card-for-card trading with other collectors over cash sales, Deckbox's trade engine still has a place — it's a trade board, not a payment marketplace, and it has no camera scanner.

How to choose

  • You need official rulings, the banlist, or to register a deck → Konami Neuron (not PathBinder).
  • You're researching cards and brewing lists → YGOPRODeck or Neuron.
  • You want to sell your Yu-Gi-Oh! from the same app you track in → PathBinder (lowest fee) or TCGplayer (biggest audience).
  • You collect across Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, Pokémon and One Piece → PathBinder, to keep one binder instead of four apps.
  • You prefer card-for-card trades → Deckbox.
