The real difference: a marketplace built in

Collectr is a strong tracker — you log your collection, watch its value, and when you want to sell, it points you to eBay or TCGplayer. That's a hand-off: you leave the app, relist the card somewhere else, re-enter the details, and pay that platform's fees.

PathBinder folds the selling in. The same card you're tracking can be listed to the marketplace in a couple of taps, and when it sells, it comes off your binder automatically. One app for the whole lifecycle — acquire, track, value, and sell — instead of a tracker plus three selling accounts.

Both track your collection for free

To be fair to Collectr: it's a polished app with a big, active community, and plenty of collectors are happy just tracking there. PathBinder's pitch isn't "Collectr is bad" — it's that if you also sell (or run a shop), you shouldn't need a separate marketplace bolted on. PathBinder's free tier gives you unlimited tracking, the scanner, and live prices with no card cap, so switching (or trying it alongside) costs nothing.

Lower selling fees than eBay or TCGplayer

When you sell through PathBinder's marketplace, the seller fee is a low single digit — 6% to 8% depending on your plan — compared with roughly 13% on eBay and TCGplayer, the two places Collectr sends you to sell. Buyers get protection through Stripe's standard dispute process, and payouts go to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule. (We're not the cheapest fee on the internet — some niche marketplaces run 0% — but against the mainstream card marketplaces most collectors actually use, it's a real saving.)

Who should switch — and who shouldn't

Switch to (or add) PathBinder if: you sell cards, run a shop or side hustle, want lower selling fees than eBay/TCGplayer, or want one app for tracking and selling across multiple games.

Stick with Collectr if: you're purely tracking, you're deep in its community, and you have no interest in selling — it's a good app for exactly that.

Frequently asked questions

Is PathBinder free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited collection tracking (no card cap), the card scanner, live market prices, and set-completion tracking. You only pay if you want to sell on the marketplace or use the vendor/shop tools.

What can PathBinder do that Collectr can't?

PathBinder has a native marketplace built in — you can list and sell cards straight from your binder without leaving the app. Collectr is a tracker that links out to eBay and TCGplayer to sell. PathBinder also offers shop inventory and point-of-sale tools on its vendor and shop tiers.

What are PathBinder's marketplace fees?

Sellers pay a low single-digit fee — 6% to 8% depending on your plan — versus roughly 13% on eBay and TCGplayer. Buyer protection runs through Stripe's standard dispute process.

Which trading card games does PathBinder support?

Pokémon (including Japanese sets), Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, and more — all tracked in one app, with master-set and reverse-holo completion.

Can I import my collection into PathBinder?

Yes. PathBinder supports bulk CSV import, so you can bring an existing collection over from a spreadsheet or export rather than re-entering it card by card.

Is PathBinder on iPhone and Android?

Yes — PathBinder runs on the web and as iOS and Android apps.
