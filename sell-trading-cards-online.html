The real trade-off: fees vs. audience

Lowest fee isn't automatically best net proceeds. eBay and TCGplayer take ~13–15%, but they put your card in front of the most buyers, which often means it sells faster and sometimes for more. A lower-fee or newer platform lets you keep more of each sale but may take longer to find a buyer. The honest way to choose:

  • Need it sold fast, or it's a hot/high-demand card? The big marketplaces' audience can outweigh their fee.
  • Selling steadily, running a shop, or moving volume? The fee gap compounds — a few points on every sale adds up to real money, which is where low-fee platforms win.
  • Selling locally? Facebook/in-person pickup is the only 0% route, at the cost of protection and reach.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest platform to sell trading cards online?

For US sellers, PathBinder (6–8% seller fee) and local Facebook Marketplace pickup (0%, cash and in person) are the cheapest. Cardmarket is cheaper (~5%) but EU-only. eBay (~14–15% all-in) and TCGplayer (~13.5–14%) charge the most but have the largest audiences.

Does eBay or TCGplayer have lower fees for cards?

They're close. TCGplayer is about 13.25% plus a per-order fee (10.75% commission + 2.5% + $0.30), and eBay's Trading Cards fee is 13.25% plus $0.30–0.40 per order, which works out to roughly 14–15% all-in on a typical single because of the flat fee. Both are far higher than PathBinder's 6–8%.

How much does it cost to sell on PathBinder?

The seller fee is 6% to 8% depending on your plan (Shop 6%, Vendor 7%, Enthusiast 8%). Card processing is shown to the buyer as a separate line item, not deducted from your payout, so on a $100 sale you keep about $92–94. Payouts go to your bank on Stripe's standard schedule.

Where do trading cards sell fastest?

eBay and TCGplayer have the largest buyer audiences, so cards generally sell fastest there despite the higher fees. Whatnot is strong for live auctions and breaks. Lower-fee or newer platforms trade some audience size for keeping more of each sale.

Are there any free ways to sell trading cards?

Local Facebook Marketplace pickup charges 0% (cash, in person), but there's no buyer protection and higher scam risk. Every shipped-order platform charges a fee to cover payment processing and protection. The lowest shipped fees are Cardmarket (EU) and PathBinder.
