# PathBinder — Growth / Listing Kit

Copy-paste copy for the directories and launch sites AI assistants pull from, plus a
submission checklist and a way to measure whether it's working. Written to match the
honest tone of the comparison pages — that honesty is what makes these citable.

---

## Reusable blurbs (use everywhere)

**One-liner (≤60 chars):**
> Track every TCG and sell your cards at half the fees.

**Short (≈50 words):**
> PathBinder is a multi-TCG collection tracker with a built-in marketplace. Track Magic,
> Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and more in one app — scanner, live prices, master-set and
> reverse-holo tracking — and sell your own cards at a 6–8% fee, about half of eBay and
> TCGplayer. Free tier is unlimited.

**Medium (≈100 words):**
> PathBinder is a trading card collection tracker and marketplace in one app. Collectors
> track Magic, Pokémon (English + Japanese), Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece and more in a single
> binder, with a phone-camera scanner, live market prices, and true master-set / reverse-holo
> variant completion. What sets it apart is the built-in marketplace: list and sell your own
> cards straight from your collection at a 6–8% seller fee — roughly half of eBay and
> TCGplayer — with sellers paid on Stripe's standard schedule (no escrow). Vendors and shops
> get inventory and point-of-sale tools. Free tier is unlimited tracking; web, iOS and Android.

## Other directories worth 20 minutes each (in priority order)

1. **AlternativeTo** (above) — biggest AI-citation payoff.
2. **Product Hunt** (above) — durable citation + launch-day traffic.
3. **App Store / Play Store listing polish** — keywords in title/subtitle ("TCG collection
   tracker & marketplace"), screenshots, and *ask happy users for reviews* (reviews are a
   heavily-used AI source).
4. **Capterra / GetApp / G2** — if you'll position the Vendor/Shop tier as software for shops,
   these rank for "card shop inventory software."
5. **Reddit wiki/sidebar tool lists** — many TCG subs have a "tools/apps" wiki; getting added
   (by a mod or a genuine contributor) is a lasting citation, not just a post.
6. **TCG community Discords** — share in #tools / #apps channels with value, not spam.
7. **YouTube** — seed 2–3 creators with a free Vendor/Shop tier for an honest demo. YouTube
   transcripts are a top AI retrieval source.

## Measurement (your GEO scoreboard)

- **UTM every link** you post: `?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=listing` etc., so you can
  see in analytics which channel actually converts.
- **Ask the AI engines monthly** and log whether PathBinder appears + how it's described:
  - "best trading card collection tracker app"
  - "app to track and sell Pokémon cards"
  - "ManaBox alternative" / "Collectr alternative"
  - "app to scan trading cards and get value"
  Try it in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google's AI Overview. If you're missing, note
  which sources the AI *did* cite — those are your next outreach targets.
- **Re-submit the sitemap** in Google Search Console after each new page so it's indexed fast.
