The apps, briefly and honestly

Tracking + selling

PathBinder

Tracks the full One Piece range — numbered sets (OP01, OP02, …), starter decks (ST), extra-booster (EB) packs and promos — in both English and Japanese, which matters because the Japanese print usually lands months ahead. It has a scanner, live prices, and a built-in marketplace where you list and sell your own One Piece cards at a 6–8% fee (about half of eBay/TCGplayer). New sets are ingested as they release. Free tier is unlimited. It's newer and smaller than the incumbents — the honest trade-off.

Portfolio analytics

Collectr

The strongest One Piece value tracker — portfolio-style charts across raw, graded and sealed, including Japanese sets. Its shop lets you buy, but there's no way to list your own cards to sell to other users, so selling happens off-platform.

Marketplace-first

TCGplayer

The largest US card marketplace, with a free tracker and scanner attached. Best if your priority is selling One Piece to the biggest buyer pool — the trade-off is seller fees around 13%, and it's English-focused.

Scanning + sports

CollX

Covers sports and TCG on one big database with a scanner and an in-app marketplace. A reasonable low-friction option if you collect across categories; One Piece depth is lighter than the dedicated multi-TCG apps. Free up to 500 items.

How to choose

  • You want to sell your One Piece from the same app you track in → PathBinder (lowest fee) or TCGplayer (biggest audience).
  • You follow Japanese sets ahead of English → PathBinder or Collectr.
  • You mainly want value charts → Collectr.
  • You collect sports cards too → CollX.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to track a One Piece Card Game collection?

One Piece is newer, so most options are multi-TCG apps. Collectr is strongest for value charts, TCGplayer for selling to a big audience, and PathBinder for tracking plus selling your own cards at a low fee — all three cover One Piece sets, starter decks and EB packs. All have free tiers.

Which apps track One Piece starter decks and EB packs?

PathBinder, Collectr and TCGplayer track the full One Piece range — numbered sets, starter decks (ST), extra-booster (EB) packs and promos. PathBinder ingests new One Piece sets as they release.

Can I track Japanese One Piece cards?

PathBinder and Collectr include Japanese One Piece sets alongside English — useful since the Japanese print often releases months ahead.

Where can I sell One Piece cards cheaply?

Among apps that track and sell in one place, PathBinder's 6–8% seller fee is well below TCGplayer's ~13% and eBay's ~13–15% all-in. TCGplayer and eBay have larger buyer audiences.

Is there a free One Piece card tracker?

Yes — Collectr, TCGplayer and PathBinder all have free tiers that cover One Piece; PathBinder's is unlimited with no card cap.
